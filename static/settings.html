Teams and star ratings

CSV header: gender,league,name,stars,code,icon. Gender is men or women; stars are 0.5–5 in half-star steps; code is 2–4 letters/digits. Icon is optional: an HTTPS image URL or an uploaded /media/ path. Club names may repeat across leagues or genders; codes must be unique within each gender. Saving replaces the catalog atomically; saved matches keep their original club details. Download CSV template.
